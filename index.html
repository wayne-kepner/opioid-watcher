Sources and limits

The refresh scripts search Google News RSS and keep each result’s publication date, publisher, headline, and link. Dated searches fill the five-year archive. The script splits searches when Google returns 100 results, then marks months that still reach that limit. Headline keyword rules assign topics and flag likely public health reporting. You need to check relevance and confirm reported policy against a primary record.

The CDC series uses predicted provisional counts from the National Vital Statistics System. News retrieval counts describe the headlines this search returned when I ran it. Google indexing may change, and the counts cannot establish total coverage, opioid use, overdose incidence, or the number of distinct events.
